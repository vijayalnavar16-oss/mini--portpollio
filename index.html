System Fundamentals

Project Overview & Objectives

Demonstrating how a single ESP32-WROOM-32 development board manages multiple active vehicle safety functions using dedicated sensors, indicators, and motor actuation.

⚙ Core System Concept

Automotive active safety relies on real-time awareness of both the internal vehicle dynamics and the surrounding external environment. This project uses one ESP32-WROOM-32 development board to demonstrate four basic automotive safety applications using sensors, LEDs, a buzzer, and DC motors.

Rather than employing multiple disparate microcontrollers or complex network buses, the entire sensing, decision-making, and actuation logic is handled concurrently on a single ESP32 microcontroller, creating an accessible, cost-effective prototype for engineering evaluation.

⚠ Engineering Constraints & Status

This system is currently in the development and planned prototyping stage. In strict accordance with the project scope:

  • ✓ Focuses strictly on the four specified safety applications.
  • ✓ Uses physical embedded hardware: ultrasonic sensors, MPU6050, LDR, L298N driver, LEDs, buzzer, and DC motors.
  • ✓ Includes a dedicated hardware Emergency Stop push button.
  • ✗ Does not include CAN communication, GPS, GSM alerts, cloud, mobile apps, AI, or ANPR.

Mini-Project Objectives

OBJECTIVE 01

Forward Collision Prevention

Implement real-time obstacle ranging using a front HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor and program the ESP32 to stop the DC motors automatically when an object is dangerously close.

OBJECTIVE 02

Lateral Blind Spot Alerting

Deploy two ultrasonic sensors on the vehicle's left and right sides to continuously detect nearby objects and signal the driver through dedicated left and right warning LEDs.

OBJECTIVE 03

Adaptive Ambient Lighting

Utilize an LDR sensor to measure ambient light levels and automatically switch headlight LEDs ON in low-light environments and OFF under sufficient daylight.

OBJECTIVE 04

Impact-Event Demonstration

Use the MPU6050 accelerometer and gyroscope to detect abnormal movement or impact-like events beyond a predefined threshold, automatically cutting motor drive and sounding the alarm.

OBJECTIVE 05

Manual Emergency Fail-Safe

Incorporate a hardwired Emergency Push Button that instantly interrupts motor propulsion and engages warning alerts, providing a guaranteed laboratory safety override.

OBJECTIVE 06

Single Microcontroller Efficiency

Demonstrate that all four active safety applications plus the emergency override can be managed reliably by a single ESP32-WROOM-32 development board.

System Subsystems

The Four Project Applications

Detailed technical specifications for the four foundational safety applications implemented on the prototype.

Application 1 Front-Facing Safety

Obstacle Detection and Automatic Stopping

Provides automatic forward collision avoidance by continuously measuring the distance to objects ahead and disengaging motor drive before physical contact occurs.

  • •
    Sensor: 1× HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor positioned facing directly forward on the chassis.
  • •
    Distance Check: ESP32 transmits trigger pulses and measures the high-state echo duration to calculate distance in centimeters.
  • •
    Motor Stopping: When the measured distance drops below the safety threshold (e.g. 15 cm), the ESP32 commands the L298N motor driver to stop both DC motors immediately.
  • •
    Acoustic Warning: A piezo buzzer activates to provide an immediate audible warning to the operator.
Application 2 Lateral Flank Safety

Blind Spot Detection

Monitors lateral blind spot zones on both sides of the vehicle to alert the driver about nearby passing objects or adjacent obstacles during turns and lane changes.

  • •
    Sensors: Two HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors placed symmetrically on the left and right sides of the chassis.
  • •
    Proximity Monitoring: Both sensors independently measure side clearance to detect nearby objects within a calibrated lateral threshold.
  • •
    Left Warning: Left LED illuminates when an obstacle is detected in the vehicle's left blind spot zone.
  • •
    Right Warning: Right LED illuminates when an obstacle is detected in the vehicle's right blind spot zone.
Application 3 Adaptive Lighting

Automatic Headlight Control

Automates vehicle headlight operation based on ambient light intensity, ensuring adequate road illumination in tunnels, underpasses, dusk, or dark conditions without manual intervention.

  • •
    Sensor: Light Dependent Resistor (LDR) sensor module measuring surrounding ambient lux level.
  • •
    Illumination Evaluation: ESP32 reads the analog/digital voltage output from the LDR module and compares it to a calibrated daylight threshold.
  • •
    Automatic ON: Headlight LEDs automatically switch ON in low-light or dark surroundings.
  • •
    Automatic OFF: Headlight LEDs automatically switch OFF when sufficient ambient daylight is detected.
Application 4 Impact Sensing Prototype

Accident Detection Demonstration

Demonstrates an embedded impact-event detection mechanism using a 6-axis inertial measurement unit (IMU) to identify sudden deceleration or shock and activate safety countermeasures.

  • •
    Sensor: MPU6050 accelerometer and gyroscope communicating via I2C to monitor 3-axis acceleration and angular rate.
  • •
    Threshold Detection: ESP32 continuously samples motion vectors to detect predefined abnormal movement, severe tilt, or shock-like spikes.
  • •
    Safety Actuation: When an abnormal movement threshold is breached, the system stops the DC motors immediately.
  • •
    Alarm Indication: The piezo buzzer and warning indicators are latched ON to signal a simulated accident condition.
Academic Prototype Notice
This is strictly a prototype impact-event demonstration designed to showcase embedded threshold triggering on an educational robotic platform. It is not an automotive-grade certified crash safety or airbag deployment system.
Hardware Safety Override
Mandatory Safety Feature

Emergency Push Button Override

To ensure laboratory test safety and immediate fail-safe intervention, the system integrates a dedicated physical Emergency Push Button directly interfaced with the ESP32.

✔
Instant Motor Operation Halt: Pressing the emergency button immediately triggers the ESP32 to inhibit the L298N motor driver, cutting motor power without software polling delay.
✔
Audio-Visual Warning Activation: Engages the piezo buzzer siren and flashes warning indicators to signal that a manual override has taken place.
✔
Hardware Fail-Safe Design: Wired using a pull-up digital GPIO circuit ensuring reliable trigger action during prototype experimentation.

Bill of Materials

Hardware Components

The project utilizes strictly the following 11 hardware components with no extraneous modules.

Component 01

ESP32-WROOM-32 DevKit V1

Central Controller Board

Primary microcontroller running all four applications, handling ADC, I2C, GPIO interrupt, and PWM motor signals.

  • Core: Dual-Core 32-bit Xtensa
  • Role: Central Processing Unit
  • Operating Voltage: 3.3V Logic / 5V USB
Component 02

3 HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensors

Front & Lateral Proximity Sensing

1 sensor allocated for forward obstacle detection (App 1) and 2 sensors for left and right blind spot detection (App 2).

  • Quantity: 3 Units (Front, Left, Right)
  • Range: 2 cm to ~400 cm
  • Acoustic Frequency: 40 kHz Ultrasound
Component 03

MPU6050 Sensor

6-Axis Accelerometer & Gyroscope

Monitors vehicle motion dynamics, orientation tilt, and sudden deceleration/shock for the accident detection demonstration (App 4).

  • Degrees of Freedom: 6-DOF (3-axis Accel + Gyro)
  • Protocol: I2C (SDA, SCL)
  • Scale Range: ±2g to ±16g
Component 04

LDR Sensor Module

Ambient Light Sensing (App 3)

Photoresistor with onboard comparator and sensitivity trimmer potentiometer to detect daytime vs low-light conditions.

  • Outputs: Analog (AO) & Digital (DO)
  • Comparator: LM393 Onboard
  • Application: Automatic Headlight Control
Component 05

L298N Motor Driver

Dual H-Bridge Motor Power Controller

Controls direction and speed of the two DC geared motors. Receives braking commands during obstacle detection, impact, or E-Stop.

  • Channels: Dual H-Bridge (2 Motors)
  • Logic Voltage: 5V Logic
  • Drive Voltage: 7V – 12V Motor Rail
Component 06

2 DC Geared Motors

Chassis Propulsion Drive

Two DC geared motors providing forward propulsion to the prototype vehicle chassis, stopped automatically upon safety hazard detection.

  • Quantity: 2 Units (Left & Right)
  • Gear Ratio: 1:48 TT Gearbox
  • Voltage: 3V – 6V DC
Component 07

Buzzer

Acoustic Warning Indicator

Provides distinct audible tones for obstacle warning (App 1), impact alert (App 4), and manual emergency stop state.

  • Type: 5V Active Piezo Buzzer
  • Signal: Digital High / PWM Beep
  • Alert Role: Audible Warning Tone
Component 08

LEDs

Headlights & Blind Spot Warning Lights

Headlight LEDs for automatic illumination (App 3), plus separate Left and Right LEDs for blind spot alerts (App 2) and warnings.

  • Headlights: High-Brightness LEDs (App 3)
  • Blind Spot: Left & Right Warning LEDs (App 2)
  • Protection: Current-Limiting Resistors
Component 09

Emergency Push Button

Manual Safety Override Switch

Dedicated momentary or latching push button that halts motor operation immediately and activates warning alerts.

  • Configuration: Normally Open / Pull-Up
  • Interface: Hardware Interrupt GPIO
  • Action: Immediate Motor Cutoff & Alert
Component 10

Battery or Suitable Power Supply

Independent DC Power Source

Supplies DC power to the L298N motor driver and DC motors, while powering the ESP32 development board safely.

  • Type: Rechargeable Li-ion / DC Pack
  • Motor Rail: 7.4V – 12V Supply
  • Logic Isolation: Common Ground with ESP32
Component 11

Connecting Wires & Chassis

Structural Framework & Wiring Harness

2WD robotic prototype car chassis with wheels, omnidirectional caster, sensor mounting brackets, and breadboard jumper wires.

  • Chassis: 2WD Acrylic Plate + Standoffs
  • Wiring: Male-Male & Male-Female Dupont
  • Mounting: Sensor & Driver Standoffs

Core hardware, firmware, and sensing technologies employed in the design and development of the system.
